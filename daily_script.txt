Did you know there is a secret highway in the United States that actually belongs to Canada? 

If you drive up to the Northwest Angle in Minnesota, the only way to get there by land is by crossing an international border. 

This tiny piece of land became part of the United States due to a mapping error during the 1783 Treaty of Paris, when British and American negotiators used a faulty map that missed the actual source of the Mississippi River. 

Today, about one hundred and twenty people live there, and if they want to call emergency services, the call sometimes gets routed to Canada first. 

Even crazier, kids living in this part of Minnesota take a school bus ride that crosses the US-Canada border twice every single day.

Did you know there is a town in America where almost everyone lives under the exact same roof?

[Visual: Aerial drone shot of the Begich Towers in Whittier, Alaska]

[Audio: Sound of wind howling]

Welcome to Whittier, Alaska. 

[Visual: Text on screen: WHITTIER, ALASKA. Population: ~270]

Nearly all two hundred and seventy residents live inside a single fourteen-story building called the Begich Towers. 

[Visual: Interior footage of a hallway, then a grocery store inside the building]

It is not just an apartment complex. Inside, you will find a police station, a grocery store, a post office, and even a church. 

[Visual: Footage of a dark, graffiti-filled tunnel]

Why? Because the town is completely isolated, connected to the rest of Alaska by just one single-lane tunnel and the freezing waters of the Prince William Sound. 

[Visual: Footage of snow covering the outside of the building]

When the weather gets brutal, residents can go to work, school, and buy groceries without ever stepping foot outside.

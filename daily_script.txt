Did you know there is an underground ocean the size of Manhattan hidden beneath the middle of America?

[Visual: Map zooms in on Tennessee, transitioning to a dark, cavernous interior]

Deep beneath Sweetwater, Tennessee, sits The Lost Sea. It is the largest underground lake in the United States. 

[Visual: Close-up of crystal-clear water showing massive rainbow trout swimming underneath]

Discovered in 1905 by a 13-year-old boy named Ben Sands, this cave system was actually used by the Confederate Army for mining saltpeter during the Civil War. 

[Visual: Tour boat gliding smoothly across the glassy, mirror-like water]

Today, visitors can take a boat ride on the vast lake, which is so deep and large that scientists still haven't found all of its underwater tunnels.

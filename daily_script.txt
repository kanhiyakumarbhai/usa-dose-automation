Did you know the United States actually has a royal palace? 
And no, it is not in Washington D.C., and it is not left over from British rule.
Head over to the island of Oahu in Hawaii, and you will find Iolani Palace.
Built in eighteen eighty-two by King Kalakaua, this was the official residence of the last two monarchs of the Hawaiian Kingdom.
It was not just a home, either.
It was one of the most advanced buildings of its time.
Iolani Palace had electricity, flush toilets, and its own telephone system years before the White House did.
Today, it stands as a preserved National Historic Landmark right in downtown Honolulu, serving as a powerful reminder of America's unique royal history.

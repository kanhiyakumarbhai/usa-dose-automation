Did you know the US government literally holds the deed to a floating hotel? 

Back in the late 1980s, American investors built a luxury five-star floating hotel and shipped it all the way to the Great Barrier Reef in Australia. 

It had almost two hundred rooms, a nightclub, tennis courts, and a helipad. 

Unfortunately, terrible weather, cyclones, and terrible marketing turned the dream into a financial disaster. 

The hotel was sold off and moved around the world before ending up in North Korea as a floating tourist attraction. 

Today, that same weird structure is rusting away in a remote Asian port, completely owned by a string of international investors with strange ties to the past.

Did you know there is a secret underground city hidden beneath the Denver International Airport? 

For decades, travelers and locals have whispered about massive tunnels, apocalyptic bunkers, and bizarre murals located deep below the baggage claims. 

The official story is that an automated baggage system was built, ran wildly over budget, suffered endless mechanical failures, and was eventually abandoned. 

Yet, the conspiracy theories persist. 

Visitors point to the airport's unusual dedication plaques featuring Masonic symbols, a menacing blue horse statue with glowing red eyes that actually killed its creator, and underground maps that allegedly show entire multi-level structures completely sealed off from the public. 

While airport officials maintain these spaces are just empty utility tunnels and storage areas, the sheer scale of the construction continues to fuel America's greatest modern urban legend.

Did you know there is a town in America where you are legally required to own a cat? 

Located in Anchorage, Alaska, the small community of Whittier is famous for one bizarre reason. Nearly all of its two hundred and seventy residents live under a single roof in a fourteen-story former military building called Begich Towers. 

Because the town is entirely self-contained—housing a police station, grocery store, church, and post office inside this one complex—getting outside is tough, especially during brutal Alaskan winters. 

To keep the indoor environment pest-free and happy, local ordinances and building rules have historically leaned heavily into cat ownership, making the feline companion a staple of this unique vertical neighborhood.

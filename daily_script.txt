Did you know there is a town in America where nobody is allowed to own a microwave?

[Visual: Aerial shot of green mountains and a massive white telescope dish nestled in a valley]

In Green Bank, West Virginia, wireless signals are strictly illegal. 

[Visual: Close-up of an old-fashioned landline telephone ringing on a wooden wall]

There are no cell towers, no Wi-Fi networks, and police officers drive around looking for unauthorized Bluetooth devices. 

[Visual: Split screen showing a scientist working with complex radio equipment and a quiet town street with no cars]

This isn't dystopian. This is the National Radio Quiet Zone. 

[Visual: Pan across the massive Robert C. Byrd Green Bank Telescope]

It surrounds the world's most powerful steerable radio telescope, which listens to whispers from deep space billions of light-years away. 

[Visual: A modern smartphone screen showing "No Service" with a red slash through the bars]

Even a tiny spark from a kitchen appliance or a text message can blur the data from the cosmos. 

[Visual: A friendly local wave goodbye to the camera on a quiet, tree-lined street]

So residents live like it is 1999 forever.
